HEOR Interactive Labs

A growing collection of interactive tutorials for learning and applying methods used in health economic modelling and health technology assessment. Each lab explains the theory, shows when the method is appropriate, and lets you change model assumptions to see their consequences directly.

What this site is for

Learn HEOR methods by interacting with the model

These labs are designed to bridge the gap between reading a methodological paper and actually understanding what a model is doing. Rather than presenting equations alone, each tutorial connects the assumptions, parameters, fitted curves, and implications for economic evaluation.

Each lab includes
  • Theoretical background and key terms
  • When the method should and should not be used
  • Published HEOR and HTA examples
  • An interactive worked example
  • Relevant methodological guidance and references

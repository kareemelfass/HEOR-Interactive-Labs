Interpretation and limits

Use the full set of relevant, mutually exclusive strategies. The analysis is deterministic and assumes a common comparable population; subgroup differences, feasibility and parameter uncertainty may change conclusions. In PSA, evaluate NMB for all strategies in each draw rather than removing base-case dominated strategies permanently. The CEF is not a cost-effectiveness acceptability frontier (CEAF), which is an uncertainty-based probability display.

3. Methods and references

Strategies are ordered by increasing effectiveness, direct dominance is removed, and the lower convex frontier is constructed by removing intermediate strategies where the next ICER is lower than the preceding one. The preferred strategy maximises net monetary benefit at the selected threshold. Exact cost-effect duplicates are reported separately; collinear options remain on the frontier. All strategies are included in the analysis regardless of plot visibility.
